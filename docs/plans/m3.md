# M3 plan — Documents and the banker workbench

**Status:** Proposed.

M3 delivers loan agreement, schedule and receipt PDFs plus an authenticated banker workbench for the M2 flow. The owner approves this plan and creates the task issues. Implementation starts after [M2 close-out](m2.md). This plan is based on the merged [Origination](../modules/origination.md#public-contract) and [Servicing](../modules/servicing.md#public-contract) contracts and the [M3 roadmap](../roadmap.md#m3--documents-module--banker-ui).

## Tasks

Use the [task brief format](../parallel-development.md#task-brief-template). Copy the dependency IDs and gate from each row into the issue metadata. Use the `m3` label for M3 issues and `m4` for M4-P. A gate of `—` lets the workflow unblock the issue when its dependencies are closed as completed.

```text
Milestone plan: docs/plans/m3.md · Depends on: M2-10, M3-P · Gate: D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6 · Size: M
```

Paths in the table denote ownership. Expand these prefixes in issue briefs and `.task-paths`:

- `D/` and `DT/` are `src/Credere.Modules.Documents/` and `tests/Credere.Modules.Documents.Tests/`.
- `A/` and `IT/` are `src/Credere.Api/` and `tests/Credere.Integration.Tests/`.
- `O/` and `S/` are `src/Credere.Modules.Origination/` and `src/Credere.Modules.Servicing/`.
